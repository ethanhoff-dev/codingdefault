Description:
Abstract:
This digital dataset represents the geology of the Smoky Mountain 30' x 60' quadrangle, Kane and San Juan Counties, Utah, and Coconino County, Arizona. Doelling originally mapped this area as part of a major study of Kane County during 1983 to 1989 (Doelling and Davis, 1989). The UGS digitized this dataset from a copy of the eastern part of the 1:100,000-scale "Geologic Map of Kane County, Utah" by Hellmut H. Doelling and Fitzhugh D. Davis, published by the Utah Geological (and Mineral) Survey in 1989 as Map 121, which was revised and hand drafted by the lead author, with minor additions from other sources. Significant modifications were made by the authors in 2005 using 1:24,000-scale color aerial photographs with limited field verification. The most significant modifications were to map the Page Sandstone across the map area and to modify contacts near the Lake Powell shoreline. On parts of the source map (UGS Map 121) the Page Sandstone is included with the Navajo Sandstone and Carmel Formation. Data is for use at scales not to exceed 1:100,000.
Purpose:
To provide basic geologic data in digital format for government, academic, and public users.

Process_Description:
This digital geologic map, and the printed version of this map (Utah Geological Survey Map 213, published in 2006), were created primarily from the eastern part of the 1:100,000-scale "Geologic Map of Kane County, Utah" by Hellmut H. Doelling and Fitzhugh D. Davis, published by the Utah Geological (and Mineral) Survey in 1989 as Map 121 and also as a plate in Bulletin 124. The area southeast of Lake Powell was extracted from the source maps listed in the bibliography. The lead author hand drafted the geologic map on a mylar base map. The authors edited and modified the map using limited field checking, and studying digital orthophotoquad maps, digital topographic base maps, and stereoscopic aerial photographs. The most significant changes were to map the Page Sandstone across the map area and to modify several contacts near the Lake Powell shoreline. On parts of the source map (UGS Map 121) the Page Sandstone is included with the Navajo Sandstone and Carmel Formation.

The UGS scanned the reviewed hand-drafted geologic map. They geographically referenced the resulting raster image, then vectorized the geologic data. The UGS modified the vector data in many areas to better-fit the digital base map, then formatted and attributed the spatial data in Arc/Info and ArcGIS. The UGS carefully reviewed color plots of the digital map to search for errors and unattributed polygons. The UGS then prepared the spatial data files and supporting documents. The geologic map version contained in the GIS files is the same as the published map except for a few minor corrected labeling errors.
